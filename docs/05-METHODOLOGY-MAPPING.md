# Methodology mapping and intellectual provenance — v0.1

**Authoritative reference source:** current accessible Notion pages, not copied wholesale into repo. These are user-curated study notes synthesizing multiple authors and examples. Preserve attribution and do not assume every heuristic is empirically validated.

1. [How to perform deep meaningful research](https://app.notion.com/p/94c20f9e1bd183998fee01ed45a81ea6): product/ideal-customer/trust research; prospect-awareness evaluation; competitors; indirect voice-of-customer (IVOC) categories; source logs; intensity/frequency; desire priority (urgency, staying power, scope); align product benefit. Maps to R0–R5 and Research Report.
2. [Como hacer una Investigación de mercado](https://app.notion.com/p/27b20f9e1bd1822f8ae281d41dc64700): Spanish-language parallel research guide; potential wording/coverage differences must be reconciled explicitly before bilingual automation.
3. [How to write a marketing campaign](https://app.notion.com/p/7f520f9e1bd1822894e901a91e3cad15): research summary; physical vs functional product; choose dominant desire; product performance mapping; awareness/sophistication; define idea; choose lead/angle; headline; body copy; CUBA; offer.
4. [Copywriting system from Agora Financial](https://app.notion.com/p/77c20f9e1bd183b197b2013da198a99e): research prospect/competition/product; Big Marketing Idea; green-light; seven lead families (Secret, Story, Problem–Solution, Prediction, Big Promise, Offer, System); direct vs indirect by awareness; copyboarding objections → persuasive subheads → body; CUBA (confusing, unbelievable, boring, awkward); offer/risk reduction. No guaranteed outcomes implied.
5. [Basic Human Desires](https://app.notion.com/p/db320f9e1bd1836b9df50110c38c97aa): supplementary psychological taxonomy; use as a lens for hypotheses, never as substitute for IVOC evidence.
6. [How to create advertising that sells](https://app.notion.com/p/e7420f9e1bd1836b9b32010ac789efdb): supplementary advertising principles; integrate after full mapping review.

## Important nuance
The user’s campaign page includes an eighth **Proclamation** lead and many additional creative angles; Agora notes describe **seven** main families. Represent `lead_family` (Agora taxonomy) separately from `lead_subtype` / `angle` (extended user campaign taxonomy). Do not silently conflate them or discard entries. A methodology version records which catalog was used.

## Methodology versioning
